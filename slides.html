NYHP Project & Operations Compass

Which building projects should a hospital do, and when? Compass scores them by the hospital's own priorities, finds the best plan the budget allows, and uses AI only to draft what a person then checks.

Artorius Reyes  |  https://github.com/AMR6958-coder/nyhp-compass  |  https://nyhp-compass.onrender.com

The Problem and Who Has It

  • A hospital's facilities vice-president and CFO hold a list of dozens of projects worth many times the budget.
  • Today: an engineering study, a spreadsheet, and a committee that ranks by argument.
  • New York's Local Law 97 adds a fine of $268 a metric ton over the emissions limit, tighter from 2030.
  • A hospital runs around the clock and uses about 4.4 times the energy of an office for its floor area (ENERGY STAR medians).

How the Agents Work

Three AI features, one model call each, no tools, no agent calling another. The plan optimizer and the fines are plain code, not AI.

Safety

Attacks
Hidden and planted instructions are stripped before the model sees a document. 11 trap documents in the repository.
Malfunction
Code looks up every quote. A rating without one is dropped; a figure resting on hidden text is refused. A person saves, or it does not count.
Overspending
$20 a login a month. At the limit the AI stops and the rest keeps working.

37 tests on these defenses pass, in a suite of 290.

Not yet done: the full trap set against the live model, and a second look at each page as a picture.
